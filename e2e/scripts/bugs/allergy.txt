Summary: Allergies are silently dropped when a patient is saved
Priority: Highest
Labels: injected-defect, playwright-agents

## Summary
Saving a patient with an allergy stores the patient with an empty allergies field. No error or warning is shown. Breaks PIQ-1 AC1.
This defect was injected on purpose for a QA demo (toggle ?bugs=allergy). It was caught by the generated Playwright tests.

## Environment
- URL: https://brycemiranda.github.io/Selenium_pytest/?bugs=allergy
- Browser: Chromium 156.0.8078.4 (Playwright 1.64.1, headless, macOS)
- Date: 2026-10-14
- App: static demo, fictional clinic, fake data only

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open https://brycemiranda.github.io/Selenium_pytest/?bugs=allergy
2. Sign in as demo / demo
3. Enter Full name "Ada Testpatient", Date of birth "1990-04-12", Allergies "Penicillin", Current medication "Lisinopril"
4. Click Save patient
5. Look at Ada Testpatient's row in the Patients table

## Expected
The Allergies column shows "Penicillin" for Ada Testpatient (AC1).

## Actual
The Allergies column is empty. No error is shown, and the form is cleared, so the nurse has no reason to suspect the allergy was lost.

## Severity vs priority
- Severity: Critical. A lost penicillin allergy can lead to a penicillin prescription and anaphylaxis, and nothing warns anyone.
- Priority: Highest. It silently corrupts safety-critical data on every save, so fix it before anything else ships.

## Frequency
Always. Both AC1 tests failed in 3 of 3 runs (6 of 6 executions). Every other test passed in the same runs.
