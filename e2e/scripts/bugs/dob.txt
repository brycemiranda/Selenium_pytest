Summary: Future date of birth is accepted and the patient is saved
Priority: High
Labels: injected-defect, playwright-agents

## Summary
A date of birth in the future (including tomorrow) is accepted. No error is shown and the patient is saved. Breaks PIQ-1 AC2.
This defect was injected on purpose for a QA demo (toggle ?bugs=dob). It was caught by the generated Playwright tests.

## Environment
- URL: https://brycemiranda.github.io/Selenium_pytest/?bugs=dob
- Browser: Chromium 156.0.8078.4 (Playwright 1.64.1, headless, macOS)
- Date: 2026-10-14
- App: static demo, fictional clinic, fake data only

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open https://brycemiranda.github.io/Selenium_pytest/?bugs=dob
2. Sign in as demo / demo
3. Enter Full name "Dee Future", Date of birth "2099-01-01", Allergies "Latex", Current medication "Aspirin"
4. Click Save patient

## Expected
A visible error "Date of birth cannot be in the future." appears and the patient is not saved (AC2).

## Actual
No error is shown. Dee Future is added to the Patients table with date of birth 2099-01-01. The same happens with tomorrow's date.

## Severity vs priority
- Severity: Major. A future birth date gives a negative or nonsense age, which breaks age- and weight-based dosing for that patient.
- Priority: High. Fix it in the current sprint. It's below the allergy bug because the bad date is visible in the patient list, while a dropped allergy leaves no trace.

## Frequency
Always. Both AC2 tests (2099-01-01 and tomorrow) failed in 3 of 3 runs (6 of 6 executions). Every other test passed in the same runs.
